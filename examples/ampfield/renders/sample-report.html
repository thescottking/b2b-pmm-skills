The morning rebuild

Before the pilot, the operations team spent the start of each day reconstructing yesterday. During the pilot that work shrank to the handful of jobs that did not close clean, and the rest of the morning went to the schedule.

“Nobody is trying to hide anything. The tech finished the job and the information had nowhere to go for four hours.”
